RB Taxi Hodonín provides reliable airport transfers from Hodonín and the surrounding region to Vienna (Schwechat), Bratislava, Brno and Prague airports. We drive 24/7, agree the price in advance and confirm the pickup time according to your flight.

Who the airport transfer is for

  • families leaving for a holiday
  • business and corporate travellers
  • early-morning departures and late arrivals
  • passengers with more luggage
  • anyone who wants the ride arranged in advance

How we plan the pickup

The pickup time is planned according to your departure time, recommended reserve, day of the week and current traffic. For early flights we recommend booking the car as soon as possible.

What to provide when booking

  • exact pickup address
  • airport and terminal if you know it
  • departure or arrival time
  • flight number for arrivals
  • number of passengers and luggage
  • request for a larger car, child seat or company invoice

Price of the transfer

The price for an exactly specified route is agreed in advance. Waiting, route changes or extra stops may adjust the final price according to the actual ride. For airport routes we recommend confirming the price and departure time in advance based on traffic, flight time and luggage.
